**Owner:** CONSOLIDARE CONTINUUM S.R.L. (IDNO 1026023120076)

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report security issues **privately**. Do **not** open a public issue, public
gist, or social-media disclosure.

Email: **igareosh@igareosh.com** (preferred for security)  
Copy (legal entity): **consolidare_continuum@proton.me**

Include: affected component/path, reproduction steps, impact, and whether
you are under NDA / company access.

## Disclosure

CONSOLIDARE CONTINUUM S.R.L. does not authorize public disclosure of
vulnerabilities or repository contents except under written agreement or
as required by law / court order.

## Scope

In scope: code and configs in this repository under an authorized access grant.  
Out of scope: social engineering of staff; physical attacks; third-party
services not operated by the company.
