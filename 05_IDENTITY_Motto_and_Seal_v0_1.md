# 05_IDENTITY_Motto and Seal (v0.1)

This document specifies the **verbal identity** (motto) and the **symbolic identity** (seal) of HESPERIA. It defines what must be preserved even as implementations, models, and interfaces change.

---

## 1. Motto

### 1.1 Motto (formal)

**Aequitas et cognitio invictae**  
Equity and understanding undefeated.

Interpretation (constraint, not poetry):

- not truth as assertion,
- not power as force,
- but fair structure (**aequitas**) and tested understanding (**cognitio**) that remain standing after pressure, contradiction, and time.

### 1.2 Motto (short, internal)

Use only where space is constrained (CLI headers, diagrams, logos):

- **Equity. Understanding. Undefeated.**
- **Equity + Understanding (Undefeated).**

Do not replace the formal motto in foundational documents.

### 1.3 Orthography and formatting

- Latin line uses sentence case: `Aequitas et cognitio invictae`
- When typeset as a ring inscription, use Roman-capitals form: `AEQVITAS ET COGNITIO INVICTAE`
- English translation is capitalized as a sentence: `Equity and understanding undefeated.`

## 2. Seal (what it encodes, not decoration)

The seal must encode the system’s doctrine in a compact, inspectable form. The seal is a constraint artifact.

### 2.1 Required elements (semantic encoding)

- **Core geometry** — hexagon / sphere fragment  
  Encodes a fixed semantic lattice: meaning has coordinates.

- **Balanced scales**  
  Encodes **aequitas**: symmetry, proportionality, rule-bound judgment.

- **Central node / light point**  
  Encodes **cognitio**: localized understanding, not vague “wisdom”.

- **Laurel (not crown)**  
  Encodes earned legitimacy, not authority by decree.

### 2.2 Deliberate absences (non-negotiable)

- no sword (coercion is not truth),
- no open eye (omniscience is a lie),
- no flame (revelation without structure burns).

### 2.3 Style constraints

The seal must look **cold**, **stable**, almost **legal-technical**:

- symmetric and proportioned,
- minimal ornament,
- no “mystic” gradients or painterly effects,
- high legibility at small sizes,
- vector-first (works in monochrome).

Mythic only in depth, not in ornament.

## 3. Ring inscription variants

Primary (preferred):

- `AEQVITAS ET COGNITIO INVICTAE`

With separators (coin/seal style):

- `AEQVITAS·ET·COGNITIO·INVICTAE`
- `AEQVITAS • ET • COGNITIO • INVICTAE`

ASCII-safe (if needed):

- `AEQUITAS ET COGNITIO INVICTAE`

If space forces a shortened ring, keep meaning intact:

- `AEQVITAS · COGNITIO · INVICTAE`

## 4. Identity constraints (what the project must not look like)

HESPERIA identity must avoid:

- warmth as a proxy for trust,
- anthropomorphic assistants,
- “omniscient” symbolism,
- heroic, militaristic, or religious ornament.

The system is an instrument. The identity should feel like an instrument.
