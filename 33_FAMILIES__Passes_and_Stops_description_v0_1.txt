HESPERIA — First Families Linking (Passes and Stops) v0.1

This file narrates the first explicit linking of Families across Areas
using border passes and safety control stops.

1. Starting Point
-----------------

- We began from:
  - 30_FAMILIES__HESPERIA_hex_canonical_v1_0.txt
  - 31_FAMILIES__area_family_table_full.txt
  - HESPERIA_FAMILIES_SETUP_V0.1.txt
- The goal was to define:
  - Semantically meaningful cross-Area passes that respect the canonical
    ring geometry.
  - A separate layer of safety redirects (control stops) for high-risk
    Families.

2. Geometry Passes v0.2 → v0.3
------------------------------

- v0.2 (intermediate, not kept as a primary file) introduced:
  - 3 passes per border between neighboring Areas on the ring.
  - CONTROL–DECEIT–TRUTH regulatory passes.
- v0.3 (current):
  - Extends to 4 passes per border (border_slot 0..3) while preserving
    ring order and semantics.
  - Uses only canonical Families as defined in the setup table.
  - Touches 95 Families out of 288 (~ one-third of the lattice).

3. Safety Control Stops v0.1
----------------------------

- We then added a distinct layer of control stops:
  - HARM → GOOD / CREATION.
  - DESTRUCTION → TRUTH / FREEDOM.
  - CHAOS → ORDER.
  - DECEIT → TRUTH.
- Each stop edge carries a concrete safety intention:
  - Redirecting from violence, exploitation, predation, or hatred
    toward protection, fairness, compassion, and community.
  - Turning destruction (damage, erasure, invasion, devastation) toward
    evidence, traceability, rights, and exit options.
  - Moving deceit (lies, conspiracism, forgery, gaslighting, clickbait,
    subterfuge) into clarity, testing, verification, and consensus.

4. Balancing the Lattice
------------------------

- After layering passes and stops we tuned counts so that:
  - Exactly half of all Families (144 / 288) have at least one external
    semantics edge (pass or stop).
  - Exactly half remain internal-only.
- Within the external half:
  - ≈ 1/3 of all Families participate in neutral geometry passes.
  - ≈ 1/5 participate in explicit safety control stops.
  - Some Families are in both sets (e.g., border Families that also
    carry safety responsibilities).

5. Combined Matrix
------------------

- We constructed HESPERIA_FAMILIES_PASSES_matrix_v0_1.csv as a single
  matrix-like view of all passes and stops:
  - One row per edge (PASS or STOP).
  - Indexed by Family global_id and Area id.
  - With a conventional weight (1.0 for PASS, 0.2 for STOP) suitable for
    numerical treatment.
- Hex and side coordinates are not duplicated here; they come from
  HESPERIA_FAMILIES_SETUP_V0.1.txt and 13_SPEC__Indexing_and_Coordinates_v1_0.txt.

6. Intended Reading Order
-------------------------

For understanding the Families linking:

1. 30_FAMILIES__HESPERIA_hex_canonical_v1_0.txt
2. 31_FAMILIES__area_family_table_full.txt
3. 32_FAMILIES__concise_spec_v2_1.txt
4. 16_SPEC__Families_Passes_and_Safety_v0_1.txt
5. 33_FAMILIES__Passes_and_Stops_description_v0_1.txt

Together, these describe the ontology, the geometry, and the first
explicit wiring of Families across Areas with both traversal and safety
in mind.
