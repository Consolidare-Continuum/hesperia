  ~20,000 relevant companies

Assumption:
- Average annual contract value (ACV): €5,000–€20,000

SAM Estimate:
- 20,000 × €10,000 (midpoint) = €200M

SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market — 1–3 years):
- Realistic capture: 50–200 companies

========================
3B — PESTLE ANALYSIS
========================

Political:
AI regulation is increasing globally (EU AI Act). Governments push for transparency, explainability, and control. This benefits deterministic systems like HESPERIA. However, geopolitical instability (especially in Eastern Europe) may slow enterprise adoption in early stages.

Economic:
Companies are investing heavily in AI but are under pressure to control costs and risks. Economic downturns shift focus from experimentation to efficiency and accountability. Solutions that reduce risk and improve decision reliability gain priority over “nice-to-have” AI tools.

Sociological:
Trust in AI is low, especially in critical domains (finance, healthcare). Users and organizations demand explainability and accountability. There is growing resistance to “black-box” AI systems, creating demand for transparent alternatives.

Technological:
Rapid AI adoption (LLMs, automation) increases system complexity. Existing tools focus on monitoring, not understanding. There is a gap for deterministic semantic analysis. However, fast-moving AI ecosystems mean integration and compatibility challenges.

Legal:
Strict regulations (GDPR, EU AI Act) require auditability, traceability, and risk control. Non-compliance leads to penalties. This creates direct demand for systems that provide structured, explainable outputs. Legal pressure is a primary market driver.

Environmental:
AI infrastructure consumes significant energy (data centers). There is increasing focus on efficiency. Deterministic systems that avoid heavy compute (vs. large probabilistic models) may have a long-term advantage in sustainability and cost efficiency.
