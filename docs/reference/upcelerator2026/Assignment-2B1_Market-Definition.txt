========================
1B — MARKET DEFINITION
========================

Industry:

HESPERIA operates across multiple overlapping industries:

- AI / Machine Learning Infrastructure
- AI Observability & Governance
- Enterprise SaaS (B2B Software)
- Risk & Compliance Technology (RegTech)
- Data Analytics & Decision Systems

This is not a single-market product. It sits at the intersection of AI infrastructure and compliance systems — where reliability matters more than performance.

--------------------------------

Initial Geographical Target:

- City: Chișinău
- Country: Moldova

Expansion Strategy:
- Phase 1: Moldova / Romania (access, network, pilot customers)
- Phase 2: EU (Germany, France, Netherlands — regulation-driven markets)

I start where access is possible, and scale where pressure (regulation + budgets) exists.

--------------------------------

Customer Type:

- B2B (strict focus)

The problem I am solving — auditability, risk, compliance — exists at the organizational level, not individual consumer level.

--------------------------------

Target Market Demographics (B2B):

Company Size:
- 50 – 5,000+ employees
- Revenue: €1M – €500M+

Type of Companies:
- FinTech companies
- Banks and financial institutions
- Insurance companies
- Healthcare providers
- Legal / compliance-heavy organizations
- AI-driven SaaS companies

Key Roles (Actual Buyers / Users):
- CTO / Head of Engineering
- Head of AI / ML
- Risk & Compliance Officers
- Product Managers (AI systems)

Behavioral Characteristics:
- Already using AI in production
- Facing regulatory or operational risk
- Spending money on monitoring, compliance, or data quality
- Have clear financial downside from incorrect AI outputs

--------------------------------

Core Market Filter:

My target customer deploys AI in production, faces regulatory pressure, and loses money from wrong outputs. If any of those three conditions is missing, they are not my market. I am deliberately narrowing to a painful, real segment — not targeting "everyone who uses AI."
