========================
4C — COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE (100 words)
========================

HESPERIA's advantage is structural, not incremental. While competitors monitor or explain probabilistic AI outputs after the fact, HESPERIA produces stable, auditable meaning by design. The engine is deterministic — same input, same output, every time — with evidence traces and trust tiers in every result. This shifts the paradigm from observing AI behavior to defining AI meaning. Competitors optimize uncertainty; HESPERIA eliminates it at the source. As Peter Thiel argues, real advantage means creating a category others cannot enter. HESPERIA occupies the unoccupied intersection of symbolic AI, semantic search, and knowledge graphs. Not a better tool — a different system.
