========================
2B — MARKET SIZE (TAM / SAM / SOM)
========================

Approach:

I use a bottom-up approach (customers × price), not top-down industry reports. This produces realistic and defensible estimates aligned with actual execution.

--------------------------------

Problem Context:

HESPERIA targets companies using AI in production where incorrect outputs create financial, legal, or operational risk.

--------------------------------

TAM — Total Addressable Market

The broader AI infrastructure and observability market is estimated at $10–15B+ globally (industry reports, top-down). My bottom-up calculation is more conservative and grounded:

- ~500,000+ companies worldwide using AI in production (SaaS, enterprise, fintech, etc.)
- Average annual spend on AI infrastructure / tooling: €5,000–€20,000

Bottom-up TAM: 500,000 × €10,000 (midpoint) = €5B

I use the €5B figure as my defensible TAM. The $10–15B industry number provides context for where the broader market is heading.

--------------------------------

SAM — Serviceable Addressable Market

Companies HESPERIA can realistically target: EU + compliance-heavy sectors.

Target segments:
- Finance, banking, insurance
- Healthcare
- Legal / regulated SaaS

Estimate: ~20,000 companies in the EU with real compliance pressure

SAM: 20,000 × €10,000 = €200M

--------------------------------

SOM — Serviceable Obtainable Market (1–3 years)

What HESPERIA can realistically capture in the early stage.

Assumptions:
- Narrow niche adoption (compliance-driven AI users)
- Enterprise B2B sales friction is high

Estimate: 50–150 companies

SOM: 100 × €10,000 = €1M ARR

--------------------------------

Rationale:

- Start narrow: compliance-heavy AI users with clear pain
- Solve a high-cost problem: risk, auditability, regulatory exposure
- Expand after validation: new industries, geographies (beachhead principle)
- Market growth is driven by regulation, not hype — this means steady, long-term demand

Investors look at TAM for market potential. I focus on SOM for execution. If I cannot name my first 20 target customers, the numbers are theoretical. I can name them — they are in fintech and regulated SaaS across Moldova and Romania.
