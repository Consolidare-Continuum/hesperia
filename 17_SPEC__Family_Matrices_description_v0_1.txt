HESPERIA — Family-Level Matrices Description v0.1

Purpose:
Describe, in purely numerical terms, how the 288 Families of the
HESPERIA sphere are linked at the family-graph level, and how the
current CSV matrices encode those links.

============================================================
1. Geometry Levels and Counts
============================================================

Areas:
- Count: 12.

Families:
- Families per Area: 24.
- Total Families: 12 × 24 = 288.
- Global family index: 0..287.

Hexes:
- Hexes per Family: 250.
- Total Hexes: 288 × 250 = 72,000.

Sides:
- Sides per Hex: 6.
- Total Sides: 72,000 × 6 = 432,000.

Derived per-region side counts:
- Sides per Family: 250 × 6 = 1,500.
- Sides per Area: 1,500 × 24 = 36,000.

Important distinction:
- 6-regular adjacency applies at the side/hex level (each hex has 6
  neighbouring sides).
- Family-level adjacency is sparser and defined explicitly by the
  specification and matrices; it is not 6-regular.

============================================================
2. Base Family Links (Canonical Neighbours)
============================================================

File: HESPERIA_FAMILIES_BASE_links_v0_1.csv

Construction:
- Source: HESPERIA_FAMILIES_SETUP_V0.1.txt.
- For each Family (288 total), four canonical neighbours are used:
  - 2 internal neighbours (previous and next Family within its Area).
  - 2 external neighbours (previous and next Area along the ring).

Rows:
- Link types:
  - INTERNAL_PRE   (288 rows).
  - INTERNAL_NEXT  (288 rows).
  - EXTERNAL_PRE   (288 rows).
  - EXTERNAL_NEXT  (288 rows).
- Total data rows: 288 × 4 = 1,152.
- Total lines including header: 1,153.

Interpretation:
- This file encodes the frozen, canonical family graph:
  - 288 nodes (Families).
  - 1,152 directed links (4 per node) at the Family level.

============================================================
3. External Passes and Stops (Edge Matrix)
============================================================

File: HESPERIA_FAMILIES_PASSES_matrix_v0_1.csv

Construction:
- Combines:
  - All PASS edges from HESPERIA_FAMILIES_PASSES_v0_3.csv.
  - All STOP edges from HESPERIA_FAMILIES_PASSES_control_stops_v0_1.csv.

Counts:
- PASS edges: 52.
- STOP edges: 37.
- Total external edges: 52 + 37 = 89.
- Lines:
  - 1 header.
  - 89 data rows.
  - Total = 90 lines.

Distinct Families participating:
- Families with at least one PASS edge: 95.
- Families with at least one STOP edge: 67.
- Families with at least one external edge of either type
  (union of PASS and STOP): 144.
- Families with no external edge (internal-only): 288 − 144 = 144.

Resulting proportions (over Families):
- Externally linked (PASS or STOP): 144 / 288 = 0.5.
- Internal-only: 144 / 288 = 0.5.
- PASS participants: 95 / 288 ≈ 0.33.
- STOP participants: 67 / 288 ≈ 0.23.

Note:
- PASS and STOP sets overlap; their counts are not disjoint partitions.

============================================================
4. Full Family Graph (Internal + External)
============================================================

File: HESPERIA_FAMILIES_FULL_matrix_v0_1.csv

Construction:
- Internal edges:
  - For each Family:
    - 1 edge to its internal previous Family (same Area).
    - 1 edge to its internal next Family (same Area).
  - Total internal edges: 288 × 2 = 576 directed INTERNAL edges.
- External edges:
  - All 89 external PASS/STOP edges from the passes matrix.

Total edges:
- INTERNAL: 576.
- PASS: 52.
- STOP: 37.
- Combined: 576 + 52 + 37 = 665 directed edges.

Lines:
- 1 header row.
- 665 data rows (edges).
- Total = 666 lines.

Graph interpretation:
- Nodes: 288 Families.
- Edges:
  - 576 INTERNAL edges (family-ring structure).
  - 89 external edges (passes and safety stops).
- This is a sparse directed graph:
  - Theoretical maximum directed edges without self-loops:
    288 × 287 = 82,656.
  - Current edges: 665 << 82,656 (strong structure, low density).

============================================================
5. Summary by Level (Values Only)
============================================================

Areas:
- Count: 12.

Families:
- Count: 288.
- External semantics (PASS or STOP): 144.
- Internal-only: 144.

Hexes:
- Count: 72,000.

Sides:
- Count: 432,000.

Base family links (canonical):
- Directed links: 1,152 (4 per Family: 2 internal ring, 2 area-hop neighbours).

External passes and stops:
- Directed external edges: 89 (52 PASS, 37 STOP).
- Families touched by any external edge: 144.

Full family graph:
- Directed edges: 665 (576 INTERNAL + 89 external).

These numbers encode all currently defined connectivity at the Family
level without relying on specific Area or Family names, and they sit
one level above the 6-regular side/hex lattice defined in the geometry
specification.
