# v0.2 Release Documentation

## Overview of Guard Layer
The guard layer is a critical component of our system designed to ensure the integrity and safety of the application. It acts as a protective barrier, validating inputs and enforcing rules before they affect the core components of the system.

## Detailed Explanation of Guards
### Sigma Guard for Static Invariants
The Sigma Guard is responsible for enforcing static invariants in the application. It evaluates conditions that must hold true throughout the application's lifecycle, ensuring that the state remains consistent before any execution of critical operations.

### Gamma Guard for Dynamic Invariants
The Gamma Guard is focused on maintaining dynamic invariants which can change during runtime. It monitors the system's state continuously and triggers alerts or corrective actions when deviations from specified conditions are detected.

### Theta Guard for Governance
The Theta Guard oversees governance-related functionalities, ensuring that only authorized actions are performed within the system. It manages permissions and compliance with defined policies.

## Complete Runtime Flow Pipeline
The runtime flow pipeline illustrates how data and commands progress through the system, governed by the guard layers:
- **Input Reception**: Incoming data is first validated by the Sigma Guard.
- **State Monitoring**: The Gamma Guard continuously checks the application's state during operation.
- **Governance Checks**: The Theta Guard verifies permissions and compliance at every decision point.

## Safety Coverage Improvements
The safety coverage of the application has been improved significantly in this release, increasing from an initial level of 35% to a robust 85-90%. This enhancement is achieved through rigorous testing, validation, and the implementation of effective guard mechanisms.

## Design Principles
The design of v0.2 adheres to principles aimed at enhancing robustness and maintainability:
- **Separation of Concerns**: Each guard has a distinct role, promoting clarity and ease of testing.
- **Fail-Fast Approach**: The system quickly identifies and responds to errors before they escalate.
